Choose the right image format

JPG is commonly used for photographs and does not support transparency. When a transparent image is converted to JPG, transparent pixels are placed on a white background. PNG is useful when transparency matters; WebP can be a compact choice for web images. If you need a PDF instead, use the Image to PDF converter.
