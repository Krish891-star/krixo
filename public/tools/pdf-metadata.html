Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is opened and edited in your browser. File contents are not sent to the KriXo backend.

Will this change the document pages?

No. Only the document information fields are changed; the visible pages stay the same.

What happens if I leave a field blank?

That metadata field is cleared in the downloaded copy.
