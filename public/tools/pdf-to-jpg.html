Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. This tool uses your browser to read and process the selected file. File contents are not sent to the KriXo backend.

Is this tool free?

Yes. The first version is free to use without an account. Browser memory and device performance may limit very large files.

Will the output meet the portal's requirements?

Not automatically. Enter the exact requirements and inspect the output. Some portals apply additional rules that a generic tool cannot detect.

Convert PDF pages to JPG, PNG or WebP images

Use this PDF to image converter when you need to share a page as a picture, create previews, or export selected pages from a document. KriXo can render PDF pages as JPG, PNG or WebP images and package the output in a ZIP download.

  1. Choose the PDF you want to convert.
  2. Select JPG, PNG or WebP as the output format.
  3. Enter page numbers or ranges, or leave the field blank to export every page.
  4. Export the images and download the ZIP file.

Resolution and page limits

Pages are rendered at a screen-friendly resolution, with up to 40 pages per job. This is useful for previews and everyday sharing, but it is not a print-production workflow. For the reverse conversion, use the JPG to PDF converter.
