Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. This tool uses your browser to read and process the selected file. File contents are not sent to the KriXo backend.

Why does compressed text look blurry?

This compressor converts every page to a JPEG image. That removes vector text and replaces it with pixels. If legibility is critical, avoid very aggressive targets or use a lossless tool instead.

What if the tool cannot reach my target?

Try a larger target, or split the PDF into smaller sections and compress each one. Very image-heavy PDFs have a natural lower bound.

Compress a PDF to a smaller file size

Use the PDF compressor to try to reduce a document to a target size such as 100 KB, 200 KB or another limit in KB. This can help when an upload form or email service sets a maximum file size.

  1. Choose the PDF you want to reduce.
  2. Enter the maximum output size in KB.
  3. Select Compress PDF and wait while the tool tries different render settings.
  4. Open the downloaded PDF and check both its size and readability.

Why PDF compression can affect quality

Strong compression rebuilds pages as images at progressively lower resolutions. Fine text, small details and selectable/searchable text can be affected. The tool makes up to five passes and may report that a target cannot be reached. Keep your original PDF and do not submit a result until you have checked it.
