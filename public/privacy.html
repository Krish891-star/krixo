Privacy, in plain language.

This policy explains how KriXo handles files, feedback and website data.

Files you choose

Core PDF and image tools process selected files locally in your browser. File contents are not intentionally transmitted to the KriXo application backend by these tools. Browser memory, device performance and browser capabilities may limit file size.

Feedback

If you submit the feedback form, your name, email address (if supplied), feedback category and message are stored in the website database so the site owner can respond or investigate an issue.

Admin and security

The administration area uses an authenticated session cookie. The server may process technical request information needed to protect the service and operate the website. Hosting providers may maintain their own infrastructure logs.

Cookies and analytics

No third-party analytics or advertising scripts are used. If that changes, this policy will be updated to describe them accurately.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the feedback form or email hello@krixo.com.

This policy describes the current behaviour of KriXo and may be updated as the service changes.
