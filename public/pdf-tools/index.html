What can you do with an online PDF tool?

Use KriXo to convert documents, combine or extract pages, reduce file size, and prepare PDFs for sharing or upload. The collection includes PDF to Word, PDF to Excel, PDF to JPG, JPG to PDF, Word to PDF and Excel to PDF converters, alongside page-management tools.

Choose a tool by the job

  • Convert a document: use PDF to Word for selectable text, PDF to Excel for text extraction into a workbook, or PDF to JPG to export pages as images.
  • Combine or reorganise: merge PDFs, split a PDF by page range, rotate pages, or extract selected pages.
  • Prepare a file: compress a PDF, add page numbers or a watermark, protect it with a password, or inspect its properties.

Privacy and conversion limits

Core PDF operations run in your browser. Conversion quality depends on the source document: scanned PDFs may need OCR, complex layouts may not convert perfectly, and image-based output may not keep selectable text. Read each tool's notes before using a result for an important submission.
