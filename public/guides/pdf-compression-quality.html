Why does PDF compression make text blurry?

Smaller files can come with a quality trade-off.

Some PDFs contain selectable text and vector graphics. Re-saving or optimising such a file may reduce its size without changing how pages are represented.

Stronger compression tools may render each page as an image, then place those images into a new PDF. This can reduce file size, especially for scanned documents, but it can remove selectable text, links, form fields and other document structure.

What to check

  • Zoom in on small print, stamps and signatures.
  • Check every page, not only the first one.
  • Confirm whether text selection is still needed.
  • Keep the original PDF as a backup.
