## Cannibalisation watchlist

1. **Page selection:** `split-pdf` (page ranges → one output PDF) vs `extract-pdf-pages` (individual pages picked → one output PDF) vs `delete-pdf-pages` (selected pages removed) vs `organize-pdf` (same pages, new order) vs `crop-pdf` (bounds) vs `rotate-pdf` (orientation). These two extraction URLs must keep distinct copy even though the output shape is the same.
2. **Markup:** `edit-pdf` (one-off stamp) vs `watermark-pdf` (repeated mark) vs `add-page-numbers` (sequential) vs `sign-pdf` (signature) vs `redact-pdf` (permanent cover) vs `flatten-pdf` (raster rendering).
3. **Text extraction:** `pdf-to-word` (docx) vs `pdf-to-text` (txt) vs `pdf-to-markdown` (md) vs `ocr-pdf` (scanned input) vs `pdf-to-excel` (tables).
4. **Security:** `protect-pdf` (add lock) vs `unlock-pdf` (remove own lock) vs `redact-pdf` (hide content) vs `flatten-pdf` (non-editable rendering).
5. **Inspection:** `pdf-info` (read) vs `pdf-metadata` (write) vs `file-checker` (images only).
6. **Size:** `image-compressor` (image KB) vs `pdf-compressor` (PDF cap) vs `image-resizer` (pixels) vs `photo-signature-resizer` (form px+KB).
7. **Conversions:** each source/output pair owns its exact term; never use “all-in-one converter” language on a pair page.
8. **Hubs:** “PDF editor”, “image editor”, “compress file”, “convert file”, “all PDF tools” belong on category hubs, not tool pages.

Highest-priority terms to validate with real SEO data when available: `resize image`, `compress PDF`, `merge PDF`, `JPG to PDF`, `PDF to Word`, `PDF to Excel`, `compress image to KB`, `OCR PDF`, `add watermark to PDF`, `edit PDF`, `sign PDF`.
